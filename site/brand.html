BRAND

Six blocks.
One gradient.

The ObenBlock mark is six blocks joined by the edges of a BlockDAG. Generations run left to right, and the middle blocks merge two parents each, as GhostDAG blocks do. One neon gradient runs through blocks and edges, from the oldest block to the newest.

01 / MARKS

The mark and the icon.

Use the mark on black. Use the icon where a square tile is needed: favicon, app icon, avatar.

02 / COLOUR

Lime to orange.

The gradient paints the mark, the highlight word of each headline, the primary button and the closing banner. Everything else stays black, white and grey.
